OUR APPROACH

How we analyse a market

A repeatable process helps separate a trading plan from a guess.

01

Higher-timeframe bias

Start with the Daily and H4 charts. Identify direction, range and important levels.

02

Market structure

Study swing highs, swing lows, BOS, CHoCH and potential liquidity areas.

03

Entry confirmation

Use lower timeframes to look for a clear reaction, displacement and a defined entry.

04

Risk management

Define invalidation, stop loss, position size and potential reward before entering.
