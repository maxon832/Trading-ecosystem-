DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

From trading idea to technical solution

A software project should begin with a precise specification, not an assumption that every trading idea can be automated.

01

Define the problem

Explain the strategy, trading conditions and problem the proposed tool should solve.

02

Assess feasibility

Review the rules, platform requirements and technical limitations before agreeing on development.

03

Develop and test

Build against the agreed specification and test functionality before considering live use.

04

Review and handover

Review the result, document its operation and discuss any agreed improvements.
